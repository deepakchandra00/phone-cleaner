SmartCare helps you review accessible storage, compare photos, create compressed copies and inspect supported device information. You do not need to create an account.

## On-device information

With your permission, SmartCare reads accessible file names, locations, sizes, dates, image contents and hashes. The storage index and hash cache stay on your device. Photo comparison reads image contents locally; compression writes separate JPEG copies. Original photos are not automatically deleted. New copies can lose camera/location metadata and have a new gallery date. Deleting selected files requires review and confirmation and may require Android approval.

Usage access permits supported app-usage and per-app data-usage statistics. Battery/network information is read for device tools. If you enable notification access, SmartCare identifies apps with dismissible notifications to let you select which current notifications to clear. It does not send notification text to a SmartCare server. Future notifications remain enabled. Reminders are optional local notifications.

Local records remain until replaced or removed by the app, Android app-data clearing or uninstallation. Saved photo copies remain in your gallery until you remove them separately. Android permissions can be revoked in system Settings. Clearing app data removes local preferences/index and may reset consent; it does not automatically remove your saved gallery files.
