Privacy Policy

App: SmartCare: Phone Cleaner • Package: com.rishi076.smartcare • Last updated: October 2026

SmartCare: Phone Cleaner ("SmartCare", "the App", "we", "us", or "our") is designed to help you analyze accessible storage, identify duplicate and large files, clean obsolete cache, and manage your device storage safely. Your privacy and data security are our highest priority.

1. On-Device Storage and File Analysis

All file scanning, duplicate comparison, and junk detection occur strictly on your device:

  • Storage Scanning: With your permission, the App reads local file names, sizes, dates, and locations to display storage usage and cleanable junk.
  • Photo & Video Comparison: The App computes local perceptual hashes of images to identify duplicates. No photo, video, or private file is ever uploaded to our servers or third-party servers for scanning.
  • File Deletion: Files are deleted only when you explicitly select and confirm them. SmartCare adheres to strict Android safety rules and never deletes personal media or documents without your direct instruction.

2. App Permissions and Their Purpose

  • Manage External Storage / Media Access: Required strictly to inspect storage directories, detect temporary cache files, obsolete APKs, and identify duplicates you choose to remove.
  • Package Usage Stats: If granted, enables showing app storage usage breakdown and uninstalled app leftovers.
  • Notification Listener Service: If granted, lets you identify and dismiss bulk notifications on your device. Notification content is never transmitted externally.
  • Post Notifications: Used only for optional, scheduled device maintenance reminders (e.g. weekly cleanup alerts).

3. Third-Party Services and Network Data

To support free features, monetization, and device utilities, the App integrates the following trusted SDKs:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Displays advertisements. Google AdMob may process device identifiers, IP addresses, and diagnostic info to provide personalized or non-personalized ads in accordance with your consent. Refer to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing & RevenueCat: Manages in-app subscriptions and pro features. Processes anonymous purchase tokens. We do not collect or store credit card or payment credentials. Refer to RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Network Speed Test: If you run the speed test utility, network requests are sent to public Cloudflare edge servers to measure bandwidth. Refer to Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention and Security

We do not maintain external user accounts, logins, or cloud databases for personal file data. All cached analysis data stays in local application sandbox storage and is wiped when you clear app data or uninstall the App.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
